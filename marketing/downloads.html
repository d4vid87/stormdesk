Choose your StormDesk.

Prefer the browser?

Open StormDesk without installing anything. Choose a location and the Graphite Silver Observatory dashboard loads with seven readings and up to ten forecast days; connect a station later if you want. The same browser remembers your theme and preferences.

Open the browser app

The new app.mystormdesk.com address has separate browser storage from stormdesk.pages.dev. Existing users need to set up or pair the new address once. The old address remains available.
