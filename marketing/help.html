Help

Get from install to your weather.

Choose a location first and the complete Weather dashboard opens. Connecting your own station is optional.

Choose how you want to start

Personal weather station

Choose your location, then open Settings → Basics → Station connection. Your own readings appear in the fixed gauges as they arrive.

Existing StormDesk host

Open the host address on another household device. Enter the short pairing code if that device needs to edit shared settings.

Weather for a place

Search a town or postcode. StormDesk opens all nine gauges and the six-day forecast; unavailable station readings remain clearly marked.

Change the appearance

Open Settings → Appearance → Theme. OLED black ships as the default; blue, light, high contrast, and orange & red are one selection away. The gauge arrangement stays locked in every theme.

Keep household alerts running

A desktop or headless StormDesk host must stay running for continuous station ingestion, archive history, alert-rule evaluation, and reliable notifications while browser pages are closed. Android by itself does not promise independent background delivery.

Common fixes

The app shows stale or cached data

Open Settings → Health Center. Check Source and Forecast first; each failing check gives a next action. Confirm the host is running and the station or internet connection is available.

A second screen can view weather but cannot change settings

That is the normal viewer mode. On the host, open Household access and create a six-digit pairing code. Enter it on the other device within ten minutes.

Official alerts or radar are unavailable

The current official-alert and radar integrations are US-first. Forecasts still work worldwide. Confirm the saved location is inside the supported region.

The Mac says StormDesk cannot be verified

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway for StormDesk.

The Linux AppImage opens a blank window

Start it with ./StormDesk_4.3.4_amd64.AppImage --browser. StormDesk will open in your regular browser.

Still stuck?

Search existing reports or open a GitHub issue. Include your operating system, StormDesk version, station connection method, and the Health Center result. Never post station tokens, passwords, or pairing codes.
