Privacy

Your weather does not need a marketing profile.

StormDesk does not require a StormDesk account, and this website does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

This website

mystormdesk.com serves static pages through Cloudflare Pages. It does not set marketing cookies, run analytics scripts, collect form submissions, or sell personal information. Cloudflare may process ordinary network data, such as IP addresses and request metadata, to deliver and protect the site under its own policies.

The StormDesk application

StormDesk stores settings and station history on your device or household host. Features you choose can contact third-party weather and notification services, including forecast providers, official alert sources, station vendors, map or radar providers, MQTT brokers, and notification endpoints. Those services receive the data needed to answer the request and operate under their own policies.

Public source and support

GitHub hosts the source code, releases, and issue tracker. Information you post to a public issue is public. Never include passwords, station tokens, editor tokens, or pairing codes.

Questions

For privacy questions or corrections to this page, open a GitHub issue.
